I am a software engineer driven by the intersection of art and code. My focus is graphics programming: rendering pipelines, shaders, real-time graphics, physics simulation, and procedural techniques, backed by a strong low-level foundation in C and C++ from the 42 curriculum.

I build products end to end, from engine-level C and WebAssembly to Android releases on Google Play, and I integrate AI and computer vision where they add real value.
